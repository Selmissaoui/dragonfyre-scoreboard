How the score works

Every component is scaled against whoever currently leads it, then weighted. Nothing here rewards sitting in a mob farm — the heaviest input is your L2Hostility difficulty level, which you cannot raise without the world getting more dangerous for you in return.

ComponentWeightWhat it measures
Boss kills are weighted by the difficulty floor the modpack itself assigns them — an Ender Dragon is worth 150 ordinary mobs, a Lich 100, a dragon 25. Those numbers come from the pack's own data, not from us.
